# Why Localized SERP Tracking Needs Residential IPs

The same keyword can produce completely different search engine results pages in Tokyo and Chicago. For teams that need to track rankings across markets, where the [collection entry point](https://www.lokiproxy.com/?utm_t=1&utm_i=52) is located directly determines whether the data is trustworthy.

## Multiple Factors at Play

Search engines return different results based on access source, language settings, and device type: local business cards, shopping modules, news items, and competitor domains all vary by region. Collecting from only one fixed location gives you "one market's version" rather than a global view.


## What Biases Does a Single Collection Entry Point Introduce?

**Ranking fluctuations misjudged**: a ranking drop in one city may actually be occurring in other regions, and single-location data cannot distinguish this;

**Regional ad placements and local cards missed**: this content only appears in specific markets;

**Distorted trend samples**: when aggregating across regions, the sample source is too concentrated, making conclusions hard to support budget and content decisions.


## The Value of Residential IPs in Multi-Region Collection

[Residential IPs](https://www.lokiproxy.com/?utm_t=1&utm_i=52) are assigned by local internet service providers and naturally carry regional attributes, allowing exit targeting by country or even city. Compared with data center IPs, residential IPs typically have a vast resource pool with broad coverage. Combined with flexible session strategies from providers, requests are more dispersed, and collection tasks are less likely to be rate-limited or interrupted due to high-frequency access in a short period. Cross-region, long-duration ranking data is therefore more continuous and more comparable.
