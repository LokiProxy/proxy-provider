# LokiProxy vs Bright Data: How Should Small and Mid-Sized Teams Choose a Residential Proxy?

For small and mid-sized teams, selecting a residential proxy often presents a dilemma: choosing a top-tier industry provider usually means facing high barriers to entry and cost pressure, while simply picking a low-cost option raises concerns about whether resource stability and IP quality can support the business.

This article combines practical business needs to objectively compare two residential proxy services that have attracted considerable attention, LokiProxy and Bright Data, helping small and mid-sized teams clarify their selection approach and find a solution that fits their own business.

## Bright Data: A Mature Enterprise-Grade Solution

Bright Data is a top-tier industry provider with a massive global IP resource network, broad coverage, comprehensive features, and a well-developed enterprise service system. It supports granular city and ASN targeting, comes with a mature management dashboard and a professional technical team, and is suitable for medium and large enterprises with sufficient budgets that run long-term, stable, large-scale projects.

Correspondingly, its pricing threshold is relatively high, with high entry costs, and many plans have relatively high minimum deposit requirements. For small and mid-sized teams with unstable business traffic, short project cycles, and limited budgets, if they are only conducting monitoring or small-batch public data collection on a phased basis, resource idle time and cost pressure can easily become issues. In addition, its feature system is complex, and new teams need a certain learning period to get up to speed, making it better suited to teams with dedicated operations and data engineers.

## Core Criteria for Small and Mid-Sized Teams

Small and mid-sized teams should prioritize three factors when purchasing residential proxies:

· Budget size

· Business stability

· Technical manpower

If your team consumes a large amount of traffic per month, has a sufficient budget, and needs high-standard enterprise services and long-term SLA guarantees, Bright Data will be a better match. If your projects are phased operations with volatile traffic, and you want to control spending while pursuing fast integration and simple maintenance, LokiProxy will better fit your needs.
