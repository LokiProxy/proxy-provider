## Web Scraping in 2026: From a Single Tool to a Layered Architecture

Today's mainstream [scraping tools](https://www.lokiproxy.com/?utm_t=1&utm_i=52) fall roughly into three categories.

**The first category: low-level automation libraries**
Tools like Playwright and Puppeteer provide browser control capabilities, but proxy management and session persistence must be implemented on your own.

**The second category: AI-enhanced scraping frameworks**
Represented by Crawl4AI and Firecrawl, these convert pages into LLM-friendly Markdown format, lowering the barrier to parsing，　but the network layer still needs to be configured separately.

## The third category: managed scraping APIs

With built-in proxy and rendering capabilities, these suit teams that prefer not to maintain infrastructure，　at the cost of a higher per-request price.

Whichever category of tool you choose, professional teams evaluate the proxy layer as independent infrastructure. The reason: tools solve how to scrape; proxies solve whether you can keep scraping.
